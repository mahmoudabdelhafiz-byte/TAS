# Your fleet, drivers and users

**Fleet & users** keeps the company's trucks, drivers and portal users. Company admins change them; dispatchers see
them.

## Trucks

**Add a truck** with its plate, in Arabic or Latin letters (both are matched at the gate). A truck the terminal
blocks shows **Blocked** and can't be booked; ask the terminal why.

## Drivers

**Add a driver** with name, **ID / Iqama number**, **Mobile** and **Messages in** (the language of the driver's
messages and app: Arabic, English, Urdu or Hindi).

The mobile number is how the driver signs in to **Yardsyncs mobile**: it must be the driver's own phone. Give drivers
the guide **Yardsyncs mobile for drivers** (on this help page, ready to print).

## Users

The company admin adds the company's portal users with **Add a user**: name, email and role (**Company admin** or
**Dispatcher**). The user gets an email to choose their own password. **Send password link** sends a new one when a
user is locked out; **Deactivate** ends a user's access at once (for someone who has left).

## Drivers' personal data

Under the personal data protection law a driver can ask for a copy of the data held about them, or for it to be
erased. In **Drivers**:

- **Copy of data** downloads a file with everything Yardsyncs holds about the driver: record, phones, visits,
  positions still kept, messages.
- **Erase personal data** removes the name, ID number, mobile, phones, positions and message addresses for good,
  after a warning and with a reason. The visits stay in the records without the person. It is refused while the
  driver has a visit under way: assign another driver or cancel the visit first. It cannot be undone.

Positions are kept only from the start of a trip until the truck leaves the terminal, and deleted after 30 days.

## API keys

If your company books from its own system (a TMS or dispatch software), the company admin opens **API keys**, enters a
name, chooses what the key is **Allowed to** do — **Book and change appointments** or **Only look** — and chooses
**Add API key**. Copy the key into your system straight away: it is shown only once. The list shows when each key was
last used; **Revoke key** stops one at once. Your IT team finds the calls in the technical page *API for trucking
companies and integrators*.
