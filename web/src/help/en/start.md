# Getting started

Yardsyncs books truck visits to the terminal and follows each truck from the road to the gate, through the yard and
out again. Trucking companies book; the terminal plans the hours, works the gate and watches the trucks; drivers use
the **Yardsyncs mobile** app.

## Signing in

1. Open the address the terminal gave you (for example `https://tas.terminal.com`).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password** and press **Sign in**.

The first time, you get an email "Your account is ready": open its link and choose your password (at least 10
characters with a letter and a digit). The link works once, for 7 days.

**Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page sends a link to your email that works once, for one hour. After five
wrong passwords the account waits a few minutes before it can be used again.

> Signing out on a shared office PC keeps others out of your account: **Sign out** is at the top of every page.

## Language

**العربية** / **English** at the top switches the whole portal. Messages to drivers go in each driver's own language
(Arabic, English, Urdu or Hindi).

## What you see

The menu on the side depends on your role:

| Role | Main screens |
|---|---|
| Company admin | Book a truck, Track containers, Bulk upload, Appointments, Fleet & users (also adds the company's users) |
| Dispatcher | Book a truck, Track containers, Bulk upload, Appointments, Fleet & users (view) |
| Gate clerk | Gate, Control room, Appointments |
| Planner | Slot board, Control room, KPIs, Capacity setup, Terminal map, and everything a company sees |
| Terminal admin | Everything a planner sees, plus companies, users and the terminal map |

If the company works at several terminals, the terminal is chosen at the top of the page.

## The words Yardsyncs uses

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Slot / arrival hour | The hour in which the truck should arrive at the gate. Each hour takes a set number of trucks. |
| Move | One thing the truck does: pick up an import container, drop an export container, return or pick up an empty, collect cargo. A truck can do two moves in one visit. |
| Appointment | One truck visit in one hour, with its moves, truck and driver. It has a number like `JED1-261020-7KQ4M`. |
| Ready to go? | The terminal's check of the containers: **Clear to go**, **Check first**, **On hold** or **Not checked**. |
| Gate code | The QR code the driver shows at the gate (in the app, or printed). |

## Getting help

The guides on this page are for each part of Yardsyncs. For an account, a password that no longer works or a
