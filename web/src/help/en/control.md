# Control room

The **Control room** follows every truck that is on its way, at the gate or inside the terminal, live on the
terminal map.

## What it shows

- **On the way** — trucks whose driver pressed "I am leaving now", with the time they are expected.
- **At the gate and on the way** — the list of trucks still outside.
- **Inside the terminal** — each truck with its stage: **Driving to the block**, **At the block**, **Leaving**, its
  stops and how long it has been inside.
- **Time inside**, **Gate to block** and **At the block** — today's averages.

## Trucks that need attention

The bar at the top counts trucks that need attention:

- **Inside longer than … min** — over the terminal's target time inside (set per terminal, for example 60 minutes).
- **No signal** — the driver's app stopped sending its position inside the terminal (phone off, app closed or no
  signal). The truck's last position stays on the map.

Click a truck to see its appointment, containers, blocks and last position.

## Positions and privacy

Positions come only from the drivers' app, only from the start of the trip until the truck leaves the terminal. They
are used for arrival times, check-in, directions and this screen, and deleted after 30 days.
