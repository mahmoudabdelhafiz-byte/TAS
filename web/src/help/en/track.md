# Tracking containers

**Track containers** shows where your import containers stand at the terminal without booking anything, and tells you
by email when one becomes ready.

## Look up containers

1. Search by **Bill of lading** or by **Container numbers** (paste them, one per line).
2. Press **Show status**.

Each container gets a status and the checks behind it:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ready to collect** | In the yard and released by customs, the line and the terminal, charges paid. It can be booked. |
| **Not in the yard yet** | Not discharged from the vessel yet. |
| **On hold** | Customs, the shipping line or the terminal holds it, or charges are unpaid. The details say which. |
| **Booked** | Already has an appointment — yours (with its number) or another company's. |
| **Collected** | Has left the terminal. |
| **Not a container number** | The number is mistyped (its check digit doesn't match). |

The columns **In yard**, **Customs**, **Line release**, **Terminal hold** and **Charges** show each check: met, a
note, or not met.

## Book the ready ones

Tick the ready containers and press **Book a truck for … ready**: the booking page opens with them filled in.

## Follow containers that aren't ready

**Follow** on a container (or **Follow the … not ready** for all of them) adds it to **Containers you follow** on the
right. Yardsyncs checks them every 15 minutes and emails you the moment one is ready to collect. A container is
followed for 30 days, or until it is collected; **Stop following** ends it earlier.
