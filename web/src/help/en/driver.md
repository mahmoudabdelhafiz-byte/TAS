# Yardsyncs mobile for drivers

A guide to print and give to drivers. The app is in Arabic, English, Urdu and Hindi.

## Install and sign in

1. Install **Yardsyncs mobile** from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone).
2. Enter the mobile number your company registered for you and press **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the SMS and press **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on this phone. Signing in on another phone signs the first one out.

Allow **notifications** (messages from the terminal) and **location** "while using the app" or "always" (so the
terminal knows when you will arrive, also with the phone locked).

## My appointments

**My appointments** shows the visits your company booked with you as the driver: day and hour, terminal, truck and
containers, and whether you may go:

| The app says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Ready to go** | Go at your time. |
| **Check before leaving** | Read the note; you may go. |
| **Do not leave** | A container is on hold. Wait for a message or call your company. |

## Leaving for the terminal

Press **I am leaving now** when the truck sets off. The terminal checks the containers once more. The first time,
the app explains what it shares: your location goes to the terminal only on the way and inside the terminal; it
stops when you leave. Press **Agree and start**.

On the way the app shows when you are expected at the gate. In the pre-gate parking you are checked in on your own:
wait to be called to the gate.

## At the gate

Open the appointment and show **Show this code at the gate** to the clerk or the scanner. The code changes every few
seconds, so a photo or screenshot of it doesn't work: show the live screen. Without a smartphone, the printed gate
code from your company works too.

After the gate you get the **In-gate ticket**: appointment, lane, gate-in time and **Your stops**.

## Inside the terminal

**Where to go** shows the block for each container (and row, bay and tier when the terminal gives them), with the
route on the map. **Spoken directions on** reads them aloud, so you don't need to look at the phone while driving.

When you reach the block the terminal knows it. Press **Done here** when the container is on or off the truck (stop
the truck first); the app then shows the next stop, and finally **Drive to the exit**.

## If something goes wrong

- **No connection to the server** — check mobile data; the app keeps the last directions.
- **Ask the terminal where to go for this container** — the container's place isn't known; ask at the gate or the
  trouble desk.
- **You were signed out** — you signed in on another phone, or your company changed your number: sign in again.
